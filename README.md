## 🚀 Start here

- 🏎️ **Karts** *(early)*: every coding agent gets its own running copy of your app and database, so it breaks that instead of the real one.
- 🗜️ **[Re:Compress](https://github.com/Kart-ing/ReCompress)**: query-aware context compression, distilled into a 1.5B model. Won The Token Company's Best Compression Model prize at the UC Berkeley AI Hackathon 2026, with Parth. ([preprint](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20787487))
- 📟 **[PingPal](https://github.com/Kart-ing/pingpal)**: teammates' ASCII faces and 90-character pings, right inside Claude Code. On npm, and I hand-installed it for about 40 people.
- 🔀 **[Multiverse](https://github.com/Kart-ing/multiverse)**: a hackathon prototype with Lance and Ujjwal. An OpenCode fork designed to try five approaches per step and keep the winner.
- 📚 **[AgentOverflow](https://github.com/Kart-ing/agentoverflow)**: Stack Overflow for AI agents. Won Elastic's prize at Cal Hacks 12.0 with Kanika, Het and Ishaan.

## 🧠 Before ChatGPT

At 16 I built NoteX, an app that turned your lectures into notes right on your device. Its summarizer became my first patent. Six years later my friend Parth and I came back to the same problem for LLMs, built Re:Compress, and won The Token Company's Best Compression Model prize at the UC Berkeley AI Hackathon.

## 💼 By day
